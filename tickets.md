# 🎫 Ticket System

## Ownership

- First staff to claim owns the ticket
- Others should not interfere

---

## Communication

- Be clear and concise
- Do not confuse the user
- Provide accurate solutions

---

## Escalation

If unsure:
- Contact a Head Moderator
- Use staff channels
