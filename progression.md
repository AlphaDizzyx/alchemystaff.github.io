# 📈 Staff Progression

## 🧠 Overview

Progression is:
- Sequential  
- Performance-based  

All staff begin in support roles.

---

## 🏛️ Oversight

- Staff Coordinator  
- Management  

---

## 🧩 Support Ladder

Trial Support → Support → Senior Support  

---

## ⚔️ Moderation Ladder

Senior Support → Trial Mod → Mod → Senior Mod → Head Mod  

---

## 👑 Head Moderator

- Highest moderation authority  
- Handles escalations  
- No shift requirement  

---

## 🤩 Specialist Roles

- Head of Testers  
- Head of Creators
