# 📏 Staff Rules

## General Conduct

- Be respectful at all times
- Do not argue in tickets
- Keep responses professional

---

## Ticket Rules

- If a ticket is claimed, **do not interfere**
- Only assist if asked
- Use staff chat for internal issues

---

## Violations

Failure to follow these rules may result in:
- Warnings
- Demotion
- Removal
